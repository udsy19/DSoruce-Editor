## F1 — G6 now asserts how much of the picture it actually measured

**`g6-renders.py:118-166` (constants), `:220-267` (`ground_regions` now returns band coverage),
`:365-386` (two hard checks).**

The segmentation only reaches what is connected to the bottom row, so the gate's *reach* was never
asserted — only that it found ≥ 5 % of frame. I added the cheap assertion the Judge said nobody had
considered: **what the gate segmented has to be a plausible amount of the frame for an eye-height
interior camera**, in two independent ways.

| assertion | 16 real renders (4 packs) | the Judge's F1 forgery | bar |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ground area, % of frame | **20.7 – 49.0** | 11.4 – 12.4 | `GROUND_MIN_AREA` **0.15** |

a real floor *recedes up the picture*. Both are computed inside the existing flood fill (`rows` set,
one line), so G6 still runs in **0.40 s**.

## F2 — four rooms must be four pictures

**`g6-renders.py:159-166` (`MIN_RENDER_HAMMING`), `:315-318` (fingerprint), `:453-467` (pairwise).**

G6 had no cross-still assertion at all, so `cp Work_stations.png Conference_room.png` passed *and
raised evidence 3/4 → 4/4*. Every still is now fingerprinted with **`G.dhash`** — the existing helper
G7 uses on video frames, not a second implementation — and all pairs must differ.

Measured pairwise Hamming over the 16 renders (24 pairs): **min 23, max 40.** A byte copy scores 0.
The bar is **10** — 2.3× below the worst real pair and 10 above a duplicate. Note the fingerprint is
taken *before* any `continue`, so a still that fails another tier is still checked for duplication;
and the rule is image-based, not `roomId`-based, because `Open_space` and `Work_stations` legitimately
declare the **same** room 193 (two views of one open workspace) and must still be two pictures.

```
[F2] cp out/renders/Work_stations.png -> Conference_room.png
    rc=1  CORRECTLY FAILS
      G6 FAIL: renders 'Work_stations' and 'Conference_room' are the same picture (dHash Hamming
      0 <= 10; distinct rooms measure 23-40). The pack must show 4 different rooms — a duplicated
      still bills one room's finishes against another room's photograph, and it would RAISE the
      evidence count while doing it
```

This is the producer-reachable one (N-1 §3: two rooms converged on literally the same camera before
`avoidEyes`). A regression that weakens `avoidEyes` now goes **red**, not greener.

## F7 / D5 — G4 checks the facade against the model

**`g4-plan-graphic.py:57-65` (constants), `:76-80` (`--ground-truth`), `:110-137` (pixel counts),
`:154-190` (the facade checks).**

G4 asserted drywall-yellow and glass-cyan *presence* and nothing about the facade it draws. It now
reads **the model** — `walls` in the ground truth sitting next to the plan, the same lengths the
workbook bills at `General!L13` = 123.20 m — and asserts, per perimeter wall type:

1. **billed ⇒ drawn**: `lengthM > 0` ⇒ that type's pixels are present;
2. **not billed ⇒ not drawn**: `lengthM == 0` ⇒ zero pixels;
3. **proportion**: the window share of the *drawn* perimeter must match the window share of the
   *billed* perimeter within **±0.15**.

Conditioned on the model, never on a producer flag — the D1/E1 failure mode. The ground-truth path
defaults to **the plan's own sibling**, so G9's three case runs are each graded against their own
model automatically (verified below); `--ground-truth` overrides it.

Calibration (all four packs, exact same counting the gate does):

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| shipped / seeded | 96.4 % (11,676 of 12,108 px) | 96.2 % (123.20 of 128.00 m) | 0.2 pt |
| dwg plate | 83.0 % (4,265 of 5,140 px) | 79.1 % (134.68 of 170.25 m) | **3.9 pt** |
| testfit | 96.4 % | 96.2 % | 0.2 pt |

## Weaknesses I consciously left open

1. **A mid-frame band of ≤ 21 % of frame still survives G6** (rows 44–65 %: ground area 20 %+, band
   coverage 64 %). The exploit's reach is down from the Judge's **41 % to ≤ 21 %**, and the surviving
   band must sit entirely below the horizon clamp *and* above a floor that still covers ≥ 60 % of the
   band rows. Closing it further means either the palette sweep (proved false-positive at up to
   24.8 % of frame — it would red the shipped `Reception`) or tightening `GROUND_BAND_COVERAGE`
   toward the worst real render's 79.8 %, which is a 1.14× margin over four plates. I judged a gate
   that reds a good render worse than a 21 % residual, which is the same trade M made and the Judge
   endorsed in kind — but this time the number is stated, not implied.
2. **The frame ABOVE the horizon clamp (`SEG_Y_MIN` = 0.45) is still unmeasured by G6.** It is wall
   and ceiling by construction, and no colour rule can police it (same measurement as above). Nothing
   in the pack asserts anything about those pixels.
3. **G4 tolerates erasing up to ~50 % of the window pixels** if they are erased to background rather
   than recoloured (share moves 96.4 → 92.9 %, inside ±15 pt); 90 % erasure fails (71.8 %), and any
   recolour to the other perimeter type fails from 50 %. A tighter rule needs px-per-metre, i.e. the
   plan's drawing scale, which is not in the ground truth and would have to come from a producer.
4. **G6's identity check is distinctness, not identification.** It proves the four stills are four
   pictures; it cannot prove `Conference_room.png` is a photograph *of room 39*, because the ground
   truth carries no room geometry (M-1 established this) and the camera pose lives only in producer
   metadata. Swapping two stills *and* their `roomId`s would still pass tiers B/C when both rooms
   share a floor family. Closing it needs room polygons in the ground truth — a product change,
   outside my lane.
5. **`--min-evidenced` is still 3, so the shipped pack has zero headroom** (F5). Untouched: it is a
   policy call about the camera, not a gate-coverage defect, and raising it would red the board on
   the shipped `Conference_room`.

**Not touched, deliberately:** every other gate, `run-all.sh`, and all product code. F3/F4/F6/F8–F11
were not in this brief and remain as the Judge filed them.
